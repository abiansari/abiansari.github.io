I am a PhD student in optimization at [Linköping University](https://liu.se), 
working on the development and analysis of Newton-type methods. My research 
focuses on modifying or approximating second-order information to obtain 
reliable and computationally efficient algorithms, with particular emphasis 
on curvature modification, quasi-Newton techniques, and Lanczos and 
conjugate-gradient formulations. More broadly, I am interested in exploiting 
connections between optimization and numerical linear algebra to develop 
scalable algorithms for large-scale problems.

I am funded by the [Wallenberg AI, Autonomous Systems and Software Program 
(WASP)](https://wasp-sweden.org) Graduate School. My supervisors are 
[Professor Elina Rönnberg](https://liu.se) at Linköping University and 
[Professor Anders Forsgren](https://www.kth.se) at KTH Royal Institute of 
Technology.

## Education

- **MSc in Mathematics**, Uppsala University, 2017–2019
- **BSc in Mathematics**, Jacobs University Bremen, 2013–2017
